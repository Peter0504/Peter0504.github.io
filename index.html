UCI team project · September 2025–May 2026

Aquatrax: Revenue Assurance & Meter Health

Co-developed an analytics platform in a four-person team to turn water-meter telemetry and billing data into a prioritized investigation queue.

The pipeline combines preprocessing, meter-level feature engineering, Isolation Forest anomaly detection, interpretable issue flags, and estimated revenue exposure.

Pythonscikit-learnAnomaly detectionFeature engineering
Method & interpretation

Features capture prolonged zero usage, communication errors, missing readings, and extreme usage. Anomaly scores are rescaled to a 0–100 risk score and paired with deterministic business rules. High scores indicate unusual behavior requiring investigation; they do not confirm a meter fault. Revenue exposure is an estimate, not realized savings.
